Grade Point privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version: your stuff stays on your phone. I don't collect anything, and I can't see your grades.

What I collect

Nothing. Grade Point has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no server of its own. I never see your name, your school, your grades or how you use the app.

Who the app talks to

  • Your school's Moodle site. That's where your courses, grades and deadlines come from. Your school already has all of this, and its own privacy policy covers it.
  • Expo, for app updates. The app checks Expo for small updates. Expo gets your phone's operating system, a random ID so it knows which update you have, and error details if an update fails to load. Nothing about you or your grades. Here's Expo's privacy policy.

That's it. The demo uses made-up data built into the app and doesn't talk to any Moodle site.

Some things open in a browser inside the app, like your school's sign-in page, an assignment you tap on, or this page. Whatever site you're on handles your data there.

Signing in

  • If you sign in with a password, it goes straight to your school's Moodle site, once, to get a sign-in token. Grade Point never saves your password.
  • If your school uses single sign-on, you sign in on your school's own page, and Grade Point never sees your password at all.
  • The sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage.

What's saved on your phone

These stay on your phone. They're never sent to me or your school:

  • Your settings, like theme, accent colour and GPA scale
  • Your school's Moodle address, so you don't have to type it again
  • Names, colours and show or hide choices for your courses
  • GPA credits, letters and terms you set
  • Each course's last percent and when you last opened it, so the app can show what changed

Your grades and deadlines aren't saved. They're only kept in memory while the app is open. What-if scores disappear when you leave that screen, and they never touch your real grades.

Exporting settings

Export settings turns your settings and course choices into text and opens your share sheet. It doesn't include your sign-in or your grades. Where you send it is up to you.

Deleting your stuff

  • Signing out deletes your sign-in token. Your settings and course choices stay, so they're back if you sign in to the same account. The demo forgets everything when you exit it.
  • Deleting the app removes everything else. On iPhone, the sign-in token can survive deleting the app, so sign out first if you want it gone too.
  • Your grades belong to your school. I can't change or delete them, so ask your school about those.

Other things

  • Grade Point isn't made by or connected to Moodle or your school.
  • It isn't meant for kids under 13.
  • If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
