Graded privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Graded is an app for viewing your own Moodle courses, grades and deadlines. It isn't affiliated with Moodle Pty Ltd or your school.

What we collect

The developer of Graded doesn't receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and no server of its own.

The one exception to what leaves your device is app updates, described below. They never include your name, school, grades or sign-in.

Who the app talks to

Your school's Moodle site. The app uses the site you enter to sign you in and to load your courses, grades and deadlines. Your school runs that site and handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Expo, for app updates. The app checks for updates through Expo, the service that delivers them. Expo may receive your device's operating system, a random token used to tell whether this copy of the app has already got an update, and diagnostic information if an update fails to load. See Expo's privacy policy.

The demo. Try the demo uses made-up data built into the app, and doesn't contact any Moodle site.

These open in a browser window inside the app, where the site you're visiting handles your data:

  • Your school's single sign-on page, if your school uses one
  • Activity pages you choose to open
  • This privacy policy

Your sign-in

  • Access token: this is stored only on your device, in the system's secure storage.
  • Password form: if your school uses one, your password goes directly to your school's Moodle site to get the token. Graded never stores it.
  • Single sign-on: if your school uses it, you sign in on your school's own pages. Graded never sees your password.

What stays on your device

These are saved only on your device, and never sent to the developer or to your school:

  • Your settings, such as theme, accent colour, how grades display and how courses sort
  • The address of your school's Moodle site, so you don't have to retype it
  • Names, colours and show or hide choices you set for your courses
  • When you last opened each course, and each course's last percent, which are used to show what changed

Grades, feedback and deadlines loaded from your school are held in memory while the app is running, and aren't saved after that. What-if scores and items you enter are also held only in memory, and are cleared when you leave that screen. They never change your real grades.

Deleting your data

  • Signing out deletes your token, along with your course names, colours, show or hide choices and change history. Your app settings and school address stay.
  • Deleting the app removes everything else it saved. On iOS, a token in secure storage can outlast deleting the app, so sign out first if you want it removed.
  • Your school's data: your grades and account belong to your school's Moodle site. Graded can't change or delete them, so contact your school with any requests.

Children

Graded isn't directed at children under 13. It only shows what your school's Moodle site already gives you access to.
